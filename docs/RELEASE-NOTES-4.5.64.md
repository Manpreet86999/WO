# Body OS 4.5.64

## Flexible workout week correction

- Saving a flexible workout through **Save & send AI report** now marks that day complete and unlocks the next day.
- Existing flexible weeks affected by the earlier issue are repaired automatically when Body OS opens: saved sessions are recognised and the correct next day becomes available.
- A duplicate-day save is blocked consistently, whether the workout is saved normally or with AI report delivery.
- Selecting a completed day now shows that day’s saved readiness and workout record instead of replacing it with the next unlocked day.

## Reliability

- Flexible-session mode and selected target muscles are preserved when a report is sent.
- Improved type safety for flexible workout and exercise-library handling.
